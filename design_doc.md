Design Doc for Ousted

Concept: A small scale colony and worker management game with a focus on realistic agriculture.

Basic Tennants:
- Real-time gameplay, not turn based events (though pausing will be possible)
- Gameplay rendered in 3D, with a moveable camera
- Dynamicly rendered terrain that supports manipulating the landscape in order to build structures
- Placeable buildings, objects, roads, etc
- 3D rendered pawns representing citizens/workers that move about the village
- An extensive farming/agriculture system
- Fully simulated day-to-day activities for each pawn
- Basic dynamic weather that has real impact on gameplay
- Seasons that have gameplay effects such as temperature, rainfall, etc
- Gameplay focused on resource management and building placement
- A trading/batering system to enhance the resouce management
- No combat system: no external enemies of any kind

In-depth details:
** Agriculture **
- A large variety of crops 
- Water management
- Pest management
- Soil fertility management - a basic "crop rotation" element will be present

** Citizens **
- Citizens will have basic needs, such as hunger, thirst, warmth, exposure, health, and happiness
- Citizens will be born, grow up, and die of old age (if they do not die sooner)
- Citizens will have jobs to work, which will be mostly automated with little player control
- Citizens can live together and have children

** Resources **
- Food will be a vital resource, can be grown or foraged
- Water will be essential as well, easier to manage but still crucial
- Basic building resources will include wood (from trees), stone (from rocks/mining), and iron (from mining)
- Additional luxary resources will be TBD, used mostly for trading

** Buildings **
- Buildings will have a resource cost (a combination of wood, stone, and iron)
- Buildings will require time and laborers to be built
