## M7 Content and art pass, step 1: shaders (2026-10-30, 0.6.1, pushed)

**Assets:** the project has no image, audio or model files (only `icon.svg`): everything is code (meshes, textures made in code, vertex colours). Art direction (plan question 5) was **not asked**: step 1 assumes the existing stylised low-poly look. Order proposed to the user: (1) shader pass: water, wind sway, terrain texture splat and snow, a palette pass; (2) UI theme and icons; (3) stockpile goods; (4) audio (procedural, or files the user supplies); (5) models and animation (improve the code-made ones, or integrate supplied ones). **Built so far:** water and wind sway. **Water:** `shaders/water.gdshader` on the water grid (`WorldVisuals._water_mat`): the vertex colour still holds the shallow-to-deep tint and the depth in alpha; added swells (vertex, none at the shore so the ground never pokes through), two scrolling ripple layers that tilt the normal (`WaterTexture`, a seamless noise made in code: slope in red and green, foam noise in blue), a foam band in the shallows, a little emission and a brightness lift (`brightness` 1.3, `glow` 0.12: without them it read dark navy next to the old azure). `WorldVisuals._fx_clock` advances with `World.fx_speed()` (so it stops when paused and runs on at the title); swell and ripple grow by 80% in full rain. **Wind sway:** `shaders/sway.gdshader` (and `sway_double.gdshader` with `cull_disabled`, for grass) share `sway_common.gdshaderinc`: the offset grows with the square of the vertex height over `height_ref`, its phase comes from the instance origin (a slow gust wave plus a fast flutter), colour is albedo times the vertex and instance colour as sRGB (matches the old standard material). `WindSway` (`scripts/world/wind_sway.gd`) makes the materials (`make(color, sway, height_ref, double_sided)`), sets colours (`set_color`, because a ShaderMaterial has no `albedo_color`) and updates every live one with the shared clock and the wind strength (1 calm, 1.8 in rain) from `WorldVisuals._update_water`; it drops materials only it still holds. `ResourceRenderer` uses it for pine tiers, oak crowns, bushes, berries, hazel and all four trunk kinds (a trunk bends by the same rule as its crown so they stay joined; rocks, ore and mushrooms are rigid), `GrassDetail` for the tufts (`GRASS_SWAY` 0.07 m). Sway amounts are the `*_SWAY` and `*_HEIGHT` constants in `ResourceRenderer`. Tests: `water_shader`, `wind_sway` (headless), `water_shot` (windowed, same map at noon, two pictures a few seconds apart: `-- <dir> <label>`; the water and about 2% of the forest pixels change between them). **Not measured:** the frame rate on the Intel UHD 620 (the notes say about 45 fps before this), the vertex cost of sway on thousands of trees. Idea: a Settings option to turn sway off. **Water retuned (user: a little too blue, a little more transparent; checked on the UHD 620: frame rate good):** `brightness` 1.0, `glow` 0, `tint_saturation` 0.95, `clarity` 0.85 (alpha scale). **Finding: in the Compatibility renderer the old `StandardMaterial3D` did not convert vertex colours from sRGB** (`vertex_color_is_srgb` had no visible effect), so the new shaders use `COLOR` as it is: a first version with `pow(COLOR, 2.2)` was darker and greyer (grass (79, 99, 94) against the old (95 to 117, 152 to 174, 91 to 110)); the sway shaders and the terrain shader were fixed, the water was retuned. Albedo uniforms with `source_color` are converted as before. **Terrain shader (built):** `shaders/terrain.gdshader` on every chunk (`Terrain._material`, a ShaderMaterial; `TerrainTexture` makes the 256 px seamless noise: red patches, green grain and rock bands, blue the snow edge). The base colour is still the vertex colour (so the soil, water and slope overlays still paint it; `Terrain._overlay_changed` sets `overlay_view` and the shader then shows it plain). **Vertex colour alpha is not opacity: it is how well snow lies** (`Terrain._snow_hold`: open ground 1, roads 0.25, fields and sites 0.5, road sites 0.6, graves and orchards 0.85, channels 0). The shader adds ground grain (`grain` 0.35), strata on steep ground (normal y under 0.86) and snow (`snow` 0 to 1: up-facing ground and the heights first, ragged edge, `COLOR.a * 1.2 + edge * 0.75` through a smoothstep, so patchy at about half cover and solid at full). **Snow cover is simulated** in `WorldVisuals.snow_cover` (not saved): builds `SNOW_BUILD_PER_HOUR` 0.18 x intensity while `Weather.is_snowing()`, melts `SNOW_MELT_PER_HOUR` 0.01 x (temperature + 1) above 0.5 C (x1.5 in rain), stays in cold clear weather, follows `World.fx_speed()`; `start_snow_cover()` at setup gives a new or loaded game `-mean temperature / 8` (so deep winter starts snowy, spring bare); the title backdrop in winter is snowy. **Winter ground is now dormant** (`GRASS_BY_SEASON[3]` is dry brown (0.4, 0.37, 0.25); the old whitening lerps with `COLOR_SNOW` are gone). The water does not freeze. Tests: `terrain_shader` (headless), `terrain_shot` (windowed, spring, half and full snow on the same map: `-- <dir> <label>`; looks right). **Not measured:** the frame rate with the terrain shader (3 texture reads per ground fragment); an idea is a Settings option to turn the ground detail off. `life_stages` failed once ("elder died of old age", a chance roll) and passed 10 reruns. **Palette pass (built):** the "washed out" feel was flat filmic contrast more than the base colours (those got brighter anyway with the vertex-colour fix). Tried ACES, filmic with a small grade and a stronger one on the same six views (`tests/palette_shot.tscn -- <dir> <label> [tonemap] [saturation] [contrast]`: spring 09, 13 and 18:30, night, autumn 13, summer 13; tonemap 0 linear, 1 reinhard, 2 filmic, 3 aces); ACES and a mild grade barely differed from the baseline, so **filmic stays and `Environment.adjustment_*` is on: `WorldVisuals.GRADE_SATURATION` 1.18 and `GRADE_CONTRAST` 1.06** (mean saturation up about 7% at noon and 25% at golden hour, shadows deeper). `COLOR_ROCK` is a touch warmer (0.47, 0.44, 0.39). Left alone: the oak spring canopy (bright lime next to the pines), the saturated autumn oranges, the night blue (the user wants a colour-temperature night). Roof and wall colours were not changed. **PINNED (user, 2026-10-30): give the shader details individual graphics settings for lesser-equipped machines** (extend `Settings` and the settings screen: water detail or waves and ripples, wind sway, ground detail and snow shading, maybe grass tufts as now; each with a cheap fallback, e.g. sway off means a plain material, ground detail off means the vertex colour only). Frame rate on the Intel UHD 620 was good after the water and sway; it still needs checking with the terrain shader (user is doing it). **Step 1 of M7 (shader pass) is done:** water, wind sway, terrain textures and snow, palette. **Next:** step 2, the UI theme and icons (the generated top-bar icons and the plain Esc menu and Citizens panels), then stockpile goods, audio (decision pending: procedural or supplied files) and models and animation (decision pending). `expansion_crew` failed once in 13 runs today (`pile (4, 4) field (4, 4)`, a map-dependent flake, not visual).

## M7 step 5b: trees (2026-10-31, 0.6.5, pushed)

**Models** are OBJ pairs `<name>_trunk.obj` and `<name>_crown.obj` in `assets/models/trees/`, made by `tools/make_trees.py` (`tools/meshlib.py` gained `limb`, a tapered round branch between two points, and `ribbon`, a two-sided strip for fronds): `pine_0..2`, `oak_0..2`, `birch_0..1`, `palm_0..2`, `dead_0..2` (trunk only), `sapling_pine`, `sapling_broad`, `sapling_palm`. Vertex colour is only shading inside a part: the sway materials give trunk and crown their colours as before (`ResourceRenderer._make_tree_layers`, `TREE_SPECS`). **Layers:** `_layers` is keyed by `"kind:variant"` (variant = `hash(cell)` mod the count, so it is stable and needs no save change) and a tree under `SAPLING_UNTIL` 0.3 growth uses its sapling model (`SAPLING_OF`; dead trees never do); crowns that fall in winter (oak, birch, broadleaf saplings) are their own layers in `_leaf_layers`, hidden by `update_foliage` (`leaves_visible()` replaces the old `_oak_leaves`). **New kinds** in `Defs.RESOURCE_NODES`: `birch`, `palm`, `dead` (`"dead": true`: 2 wood, never grows). **Climate species** (`Climate.DEFS` `species`, `dead`; `Climate.pick_tree(patch, cell)` walks the species list by the species noise so kinds grow in patches and neighbours in the list mix at the edges; dead trees come from a hash of the cell, so world generation uses the same random numbers as before; `Climate.plant_kind(roll)` is what woodcutters plant): temperate pine .45, birch .17, oak .38, 1.5% dead; arctic pine .72, birch .28, 7% dead; desert palm, 50% dead; tropical palm .5, oak .5. **Snow on trees:** `sway.gdshader` has `snow`, `snow_hold` and `snow_color`; `WindSway.update(clock, wind, snow)` sets the cover on every material, `WindSway.hold_snow(mat)` marks pine boughs and bushes as snow-holding. **Felling:** `ResourceRenderer.fell(rn, from)` (called from `TaskBase.harvest_node_task` when a chopped tree is used up, before `remove_node`) adds a `FallingTree` (`scripts/world/falling_tree.gd`): the tree's own meshes, tipping about the foot away from the woodcutter over `FALL_SECONDS` 1.25 with a rebound, lying 0.9 s, sinking 0.9 s; runs on `World.fx_speed()` (held when paused; capped at 3x); plays `treefall.wav` (`tools/make_sounds.py`, generated last so the other sounds do not change). Cleared and replanted saplings do not fall. Tests: `tree_models` (headless), `tree_shot` (windowed: rows of every kind, a growth row, summer, autumn, snowy winter), `fell_shot` (windowed, pictures at intervals of three felled trees). **Not done in this slice:** orchard trees with fruit, bushes, rocks, ore, mushrooms, ground plants as asset models; a tree-detail setting and distance levels of detail; the frame rate with the new meshes (about the same triangle count per tree as before, more variants so more draw calls) is unmeasured on the UHD 620.

## M7 step 5d: crop models (2026-10-31, 0.6.7, pushed)

**Models** (`tools/make_crops.py`, OBJ with the plant's own vertex colours in `assets/models/crops/`): per crop `crop_<crop>_young`, `_grown`, `_ripe` for wheat, barley, beans, potato, radish, lettuce, cabbage and pumpkin, and per plant class (`CropMeshes.SHAPE_OF`: grain, leafy, bush, vine) `crop_sprout_<class>` and `crop_dead_<class>`. **Picking one:** `CropMeshes.crop_key(crop, state, growth)`: DEAD is the class's withered plot, RIPE the ripe model, otherwise growth under `SEEDLING_UNTIL` 0.25 is the seedling, under `YOUNG_UNTIL` 0.6 young, then grown (frozen plots keep their stage and are tinted); `stage_progress` scales a model from 85% to full inside its stage. `Field._rebuild_crops` groups plots by key and fills one lazily-made multimesh layer per key in use (`_crop_layers`, emptying those no plot uses), tinting by instance colour for frost and pests only; the material is `CropMeshes.crop_material()` (a double-sided sway material, so crops follow the grass sway setting). The old code-built plant meshes, `CropMeshes.SHAPES` and `mesh_for` are gone (the orchard trees are models since 5c; `CropMeshes.material()` stays for the pasture animals). Tests: `crop_models` (headless), `crops_shot` (windowed: a field with a column per crop and a row per stage: seedling, young, grown, ripe, frozen, withered, pest-hit). **Not done:** a second look at crop colours against the soil overlay, tuning the plot size so crops fill the cell (each is a clump about 0.8 wide), frame rate on the UHD 620 (a ripe wheat plot is about 300 triangles and barley 400, against about 250 for the old clump; a 16x16 field of barley is about 100,000), a setting to thin the crops.

## M7 step 6: tree and crop detail settings (2026-10-31, 0.6.11, pushed)

**Measured first** (a headless count over temperate seeds 1 to 3): about 1.04 to 1.09 million triangles of nature per map (trees, bushes, rocks, hazel, mushrooms), against about 0.5 million for the old code-built meshes; a ripe crop plot is 120 to 420 triangles.
