## M8 tavern (2026-11-01, 0.7.1-dev, not pushed)

`tavern` def (Civic, 4x3, **wood 40, stone 20, iron 4**, work 36: the costliest ordinary building by trade value; `"tavern": true`, `patrons` 10, `inputs {spirits: 8}` + `keep_inputs` + `"policy": &"treats"`, control `keep_spirits` (per-building level: `Building.inputs_wanted` now reads `settings["keep_<item>"]` and returns nothing when the def's `policy` is off); model `cottage("tavern", ...)` in `make_buildings.py`, icon `tavern.svg`). **Visits:** `NeedsTasks.tavern_task` (in `JobManager.choose_task` after the treat block, adults, daytime): between `Defs.TAVERN_FROM` 17:00 and `TAVERN_TO` 20:00 each adult decides once a day (`Pawn.tavern_day`, saved): `TAVERN_SHARE` 55% go (a hash of citizen and day, no rng), walk to the nearest tavern with room, go inside for `TAVERN_STAY` 1.5 h, come out with `tavern_until` +20 h (the mood factor "Evening at the tavern", `TAVERN_MOOD` +0.04: very minor, no spirits needed) and, if the treats policy is on, the tavern has spirits and they `wants_treat()`, drink one from the tavern's `inputs` (`enjoy_treat`, the normal spirits boost).
