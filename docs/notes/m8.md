## M8 root cellar (2026-11-01, pushed as 0.7.1)

`root_cellar` def (Storage, 3x3, wood 6, stone 16, work 18, food only, **capacity 150**, `spoil_mult` 0.3, **`ground_temp` 9 and `ground_share` 0.25**). `Building.storage_temp()` is the air temperature a store spoils food at: the air for ordinary stores, otherwise `ground_temp + share x (air - ground_temp)`; `spoil_tick` uses it. So a cellar spoils about a third as much as the open at 10 C, far less than a granary in a hot summer (28 C: 0.41 against 1.4 of the open rate), and in deep winter more than a frozen granary (which spoils almost nothing at -5 C, the cellar sits at about 5.5 C), which is the trade-off. `storage_rank` still uses `spoil_mult` only (a cellar outranks a granary all year; a seasonal rank would make food shuttle between stores). Model `root_cellar` (own function in `make_buildings.py`: a stone front wall 1.3 m high with an arched opening cut in columns, a pale arch ring, a low stone kerb, and a grassy mound as the roof mesh (an ellipsoid); the door is an arched plank door with two iron bands; `roof_texture` def key picks the roof's texture, new `turf` kind in `SurfaceTextures`) and hand-drawn icon. Test `root_cellar`. **Not done:** a cellar upgrade for a stockpile or a house, a seasonal storage choice, ice (an ice house), spoilage shown per store in the inspector beyond the help text, a look at the model in the windowed shots.
