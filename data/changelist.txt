# 0.4.2 | 28 Oct 2026 | Trade: merchants, barter, seeds and animals (M6 steps 1 to 3)
- A Trading Post, built as an open market stall, and merchants who walk in from the map edge with a donkey cart a few times a season, stay a few days and leave.
- A barter window: they sell goods at 1.25 times their value and pay full value (times their own price multiplier) for what they asked for, half for anything else. Citizens carry what you sell to the post and what you buy to storage, and nothing changes hands until it has all arrived.
- Merchants sell seeds and saplings for crops you have not found yet (the ones you can sow now are offered more often), so trade is now the main way to new crops.
- Pastures and chicken runs start empty: buy animals from a merchant and a citizen leads them to their pasture.
- The Cow Pasture and Chicken Run no longer come with animals.

# 0.4.1 | 28 Oct 2026 | Visual pass
- The top bar no longer cuts off the Homeless count.
- Staked road cells are now a flat, worn patch that clearly differs from a finished road.
- The changelist panel no longer covers the menu buttons.

# 0.4.0 | 28 Oct 2026 | Agriculture depth: M4 complete (steps 4 to 8)
- Soil view: a Soil button in the top bar colours the ground by fertility (brown poor, green rich), numbers each plot of the selected field, and the crop list warns when a crop would drain poor soil or points out ones that restore it.
- Water: random droughts (no rain, hotter, everything dries fast), buckets (farmers fetch water from a well for dry plots), and irrigation channels dug from a river or lake that keep nearby plots moist.
- Pests: outbreaks that spread between plots, likelier when the same crop grows in several fields nearby; farmers pick them off, a frost clears them, and a Scarecrow field upgrade makes them rare.
- Orchards: apple and pear trees, planted once and picked every year. Saplings are found by foragers, and no orchard can be placed until one is found.
- Livestock: a Chicken Run (eggs) and a Cow Pasture (milk). Animals graze in warm weather, need grain in the cold months, breed up to the room, and starve without feed.
- Manure: animals leave it, farmers collect and spread it on poor fallow plots before sowing.
- The title screen has a Changelist button (this list) and shows the build id in the lower right.

# 0.3.2 | 28 Oct 2026 | Playtest fixes, round two
- Buildings can be rotated with R: the door, chimney and shapes turn with them.
- Fields, graveyards and stockpiles start as a single tile you drag out; anything under 2x2 is refused. The field fence is lower.
- Staked roads look like faintly worn grass, not like built roads. Road stones are smaller.
- The Citizens panel sorts by any column. The 4 key sets 10x speed.
- The game starts with 12 citizens, 3 of them children. Surface stone is down to 2x.
- Couples who ended up in different houses now move in together, so babies are born again (found from a real save).
- Farmers deliver food to the Granary instead of the nearest store; plants marked for clearing no longer wait for storage room.
- Crops can be sown whenever it is warm enough, ripening or not (early harvest and frost salvage cover the risk).
- The Woodcutter has a Work first setting (firewood by default). Blacksmith and School workers go inside while they work.
- The Accepts filter is a compact list, and long inspector text is behind a ? button.
- The moon crosses the sky the same way as the sun, outdoor sleepers lie down, hazel looks different from berry bushes, and the blacksmith window no longer smears.

# 0.3.1 | 27 Oct 2026 | The playtest notes pass
- Mechanics: 10x speed, shacks always on, the Gatherer profession retired (laborers gather), roads cost work instead of materials, and at least five loose rocks near the start.
- Expansion: fields, stockpiles and graveyards can be enlarged after they are built.
- Crops: sowing by climate instead of season lists, early harvest, frost salvage, and a spoilage log that names what spoiled.
- Idle professionals now work as laborers.
- Look: building silhouettes (silo Granary, Town Hall tower, forge, school bell tower, mine headframe), fences, stockpile crates, graveyard ground, furrowed soil, work and walking animations, procedural wall and roof textures.
- Interface: an Esc menu, a Policies panel, a building ghost showing the real building, a Citizens panel, idle tags, a worker list, a Cancel tool and quieter site labels.

# 0.3.0 | 26 Oct 2026 | The first push: M1 to M3 done, M4 begun
- M1: the core loop. Terrain, camera, weather, seasons, citizens with needs, construction, roads, fields and a day and night cycle.
- M2: logistics and economy. Per-building storage, spoilage, woodcutter, house heating, mine, coal, tools and blacksmith, building upgrades, save and load.
- M3: population. Ages and schools, job experience, families and births, happiness and grief, graveyards, homelessness and shacks, a Town Hall that grows by upgrades, difficulty presets, a governance ledger.
- M4 so far: eight crops, foraging (mushrooms, hazel, the Forager's Hut), and seeds found through play.
- A title screen with a mock-town backdrop, chimney smoke, grass tufts and richer trees.
